About Me

Statistics that move beyond the classroom.

I’m an Applied Statistics undergraduate at the Faculty of Science, University of Colombo, interested in turning statistical and mathematical methods into practical solutions for real-world problems.

My work sits at the intersection of statistical modelling, optimization, data analytics, and software development. I enjoy taking a problem, understanding the underlying data, building a quantitative model, validating the results, and then turning the analysis into something people can actually use.

My projects span extreme-value modelling, time-series forecasting, predictive analytics, route optimization, financial simulation, sports analytics, and interactive data visualization.

How I Work

01
